Architecture Overview

This project models the complete data engineering lifecycle of an enterprise e-commerce platform.

The lifecycle begins with data generation from three primary sources:

Transactional databases (orders, customers, products, and payments)
Clickstream events generated by customer interactions
Third-party inventory log streams

The generated data is collected by the ingestion layer through APIs and streaming services, where validation and preprocessing are performed.

Validated data is stored in a Raw Data Lake before undergoing transformation processes such as cleaning, deduplication, aggregation, and enrichment. The processed data is then loaded into a Data Warehouse.

Finally, the serving layer delivers processed information to business dashboards, analytics reports, APIs, recommendation engines, and machine learning applications.

Throughout the entire lifecycle, Security, Observability, and Data Privacy are enforced to ensure reliable, secure, and compliant data processing.
